KeepChangeFlip

Turn a subtraction problem into an addition problem.

First number
5
Second number
3

Now add

Why does this work?

Subtracting a number gives the same answer as adding its opposite.

5 − 3 and 5 + (−3) both move you 3 steps left from 5.

5 − (−3) and 5 + 3 both move you 3 steps right from 5.

That is why we Change − to + and Flip the second number.

Need a reminder?

Keep the first number the same.

Change − to +.

Flip the sign of the second number. Positive becomes negative. Negative becomes positive.

Then add. Adding a positive number moves right. Adding a negative number moves left.
